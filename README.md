# Lyra — Task Planning Bot for Singapore O-Level Computing

## 1. Introduction and Setup  
Lyra is a task planning bot that specializes in the Singapore O-Level computing syllabus, equipped with the official textbook.  
Visit https://lyra72.streamlit.app

---

## 2. Getting Started

- If the app has fallen asleep due to inactivity, click "Yes, get this app back up". Then, you will see the log in page.  
- If you do not have an account, you can create one.  
- Press the dropdown under 'Do you have an account' and press no. Enter your email and a password.  
- Afterwards, check you email, including your spam, for a sign up link. Ensure the email is from Lyra, and do not worry if the email is flagged as suspicious.  
- Then, follow the instructions to sign up, and you will be brought to the home page.

- If you have an account, enter your email and password, then press Log In. This also brings you to the home page.

---

## 3. Navigating the app

- When you reach the home page, there are several things to take note of. The gear icon on the top right will bring you to the settings page where you can log out or delete your account. The main views allow you to see the study plans that you have. Finally, there is a button to create a study plan.  
- When you press the create study plan button, you are brought to a page where you can enter the specific computing chapters and subchapters you would like to study.  
- You can add more tasks by pressing, "Add another one?", or delete one by pressing the "X" icon.  
- When editing the task details before prompting, you can include how confident you are feeling and any other notes you would like to add when generating these tasks.  
- When you are done, press the create study plan button. Press yes, then press send prompt. There will be a random GIF available to pass time while loading.

- Afterwards, the study tasks will be generated, detailing how long you should spend on the task and by when.  
- You can change this info later in the home page.  
- If you are satisfied, you can press the home button on the top left, indicated by the house emoji. Otherwise, you can press the back arrow emoji to change the specifics of the study plan.

- Going back to the home page, you can see that the blue part has been populated if it was not done so before. You can edit the task by pressing on the toggle. Then, press save to save your changes.  
- You can also easily access more information of your task by pressing the toggle of see more.
